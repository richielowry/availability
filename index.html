Get in touch How it works

Who I can help

Every child learns differently. Tuition is tailored to the individual rather than squeezed into a one-size-fits-all lesson plan.

Children on the autism spectrum

Structured, predictable sessions with visual supports and a gentle pace, built around how your child learns best.

Additional learning needs

Support for children with literacy or numeracy difficulties, or those who need extra confidence and consolidation.

Children out of school

Continuity of learning for children who are awaiting a school place or are missing school for medical or other reasons.

Catch-up & transition

Help to bridge gaps in Irish, English and Maths, or to prepare for a move to a new class or school.

How it works

1

Introductory chat

We talk about your child, what's working, what isn't, and what you'd like to achieve. No obligation.

2

A personal learning plan

I put together clear, achievable goals that link to the primary curriculum and your child's own interests.

3

Regular sessions at home

Relaxed one-to-one sessions in a familiar space, at a time that suits your family.

4

Ongoing feedback

Short updates after sessions and regular progress reviews so you always know how things are going.

What a session might look like

Settle in

A familiar routine and a visual schedule so your child knows what to expect.

Focus work

Short, well-paced activities in literacy, numeracy or the area we're targeting.

Learning through play

Games, hands-on materials and your child's favourite interests keep learning enjoyable.

Wrap up

A positive finish, with a note home on what we covered and what to try next.
